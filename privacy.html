Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 5, 2026

DrawAFish.com is a small community site for drawing and voting on fish. This page explains what information we handle and why.

What we collect

  • Drawings and artist names you submit are public.
  • Account details (email address) if you choose to create an account.
  • IP addresses and browser information (user agent) when you submit or vote on fish. We keep these in moderation logs to prevent abuse — for example, detecting and undoing bot voting — and we don't use them for anything else.
  • Basic analytics via Google Analytics, per Google's Privacy Policy.

Bot protection (Cloudflare Turnstile)

To keep voting fair, we use Cloudflare Turnstile, which runs invisibly when you vote and helps us tell people apart from bots. Turnstile evaluates limited device and network signals on Cloudflare's servers to make that determination. Cloudflare's handling of this data is described in the Cloudflare Turnstile Privacy Addendum and the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data.
  • We don't share moderation logs with anyone except as required to operate the site or by law.
